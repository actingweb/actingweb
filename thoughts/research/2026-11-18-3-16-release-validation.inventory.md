One row per consumer-visible change in the merges to `master` since
`v3.15.1`, built from each merge's own changelog hunk
(`git diff <merge>^1 <merge> -- CHANGELOG.rst`) and checked against the
tree at `da6de67`. **Line numbers are `CHANGELOG.rst` and
`docs/migration/v3.16.rst` at `da6de67`**, before this plan's
corrections moved them. "Meets an upgrader who did nothing" is one of
*refuses-to-start*, *error-at-runtime*, *silent* (behaviour differs with no
error), *irreversible* (a 3.15.1 process cannot read what the release
wrote) or *none*. The guide column is as found; Phase 4 fills what is
`NONE`.
