## Issues Found

### 1. A storage fault on one empty property stops the rest of the grant's sync
**Severity:** Low (a storage fault, not peer-triggered; every write in the
loop already aborted the batch the same way: `set_value`, `set_list` and the
deletes raise `DbError` through `callbacks.py:1938`)
**Description:** The strict read adds one more source of that abort: the
patterns after it in `_incremental_sync_granted_properties` are not fetched,
and only the caller's error log says so, until a full sync.
**Location:** `actingweb/handlers/callbacks.py:1813-1821` (the loop, no
per-pattern handling); `:1880` the new strict read.
**Recommendation:** catch `DbError` per pattern, log it with the property
name and go on, if the owner wants the batch to be best-effort.

### 2. `list_all_lists` is a walk with a documented gap; `exists()` is a point read
**Severity:** Low
**Description:** The strict walk reads every list the replica holds for one
name, and misses a pre-3.16 list whose name continues another's with `-#`
(`attribute_list_store.py:81-84`), which then stays behind as before.
`AttributeListStore.exists(name)` (`attribute_list_store.py:60`, "one or
two point reads ... A fault raises DbError") has neither problem;
`RemotePeerStore` does not expose it.
**Location:** `actingweb/handlers/callbacks.py:1880`
**Recommendation:** a `RemotePeerStore.list_exists(name)` delegating to
`exists()`.

### 3. The uncached read no longer clears a cached entry on its `None`-without-caching paths
**Severity:** Low (owner-reachable only)
**Description:** With no bucket (`trust_permissions.py:626` "`if not bucket:`")
or a stored override that does not parse (the `except Exception` return),
`_read_permissions` answers `None` and leaves an older cached override in
place; the removed `pop` used to clear it. The owner's DELETE of the grant
then answers 404 while `get_permissions` applies the cached one for up to
300 s.
**Location:** `actingweb/trust_permissions.py:626`, and the `except
Exception` branch of `_read_permissions`.
**Recommendation:** pop the key on those two paths.

### 4. A slow read can overwrite a newer store's cache entry
**Severity:** Low (predates the plan on the `get_permissions` miss path; one
round-trip window, bounded by the 300 s TTL)
**Location:** `actingweb/trust_permissions.py` `_read_permissions`,
"`self._cache[cache_key] = permissions`"
**Recommendation:** a per-key generation counter bumped by store and delete,
checked before caching. Belongs with `mcp-cache-lifecycle-and-revocation.md`
section 1 if taken.

### 5. The web UI override copies the base method and matches a literal
**Severity:** Low (hygiene)
**Location:** `actingweb/handlers/www.py:116` and its
`"Invalid username or password"` comparison (set at `actingweb/auth.py:166,171`)
**Recommendation:** a constant in `auth.py` both sides import.
